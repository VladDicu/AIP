# AIP – AI Educational Platform

AIP (AI-powered Platform) is designed to centralize and structure student learning resources in one reliable space.

## Problem Addressed
Students currently spread their learning across course notes, academic papers, websites, and external tools, making study workflows fragmented. While general-purpose AI tools can help, they may return unverified or inaccurate information when used without critical evaluation.

## Core Objectives
- Provide a centralized platform for educational content management.
- Use AI in a structured, responsible way for study support.
- Separate **teacher-verified** resources from external resources through a **strict source mode**.

## Platform Capabilities
- Organizes course materials by subject and learning context.
- Supports teacher-uploaded and verified resources.
- Integrates external resources when strict source mode is disabled.
- Computes and displays a confidence score when multi-source generation is used.
- Generates summaries, learning plans, quizzes, and exercises from available materials.
- Converts course content into audio (podcast-like format) to support comprehension and reduce cognitive fatigue.
- Supports educational browser extensions for:
  - grammar and writing support,
  - productivity and time management,
  - language learning (translation and vocabulary assistance).
- Includes accessibility features such as:
  - text-to-speech,
  - adjustable display settings for learners with visual or learning difficulties.
- Provides teacher-facing analytics to review student responses and identify strengths and weaknesses faster.

## Educational Impact
AIP improves reliability, efficiency, personalization, and engagement in digital education while encouraging responsible AI use and critical thinking.

## Keywords
Artificial Intelligence, education, personalized learning, accessibility, digital platform.
